Overview

Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help users explore national parks and plan trips based on their interests, location, and preferred outdoor activities. The site will make it easier for visitors to compare parks, learn basic information, and build a simple travel plan in one place.

Audience

The intended audience includes travelers, families, students, and outdoor enthusiasts who want a simple and organized way to research national parks. It is especially useful for people who enjoy hiking, camping, sightseeing, and planning nature-focused vacations.

Dynamic elements

JavaScript will be used to make the site interactive by allowing users to filter parks by activity, region, or difficulty level. Users will also be able to click buttons to view more details, save favorite parks, and receive suggested trip options based on their selections. The site will use arrays and objects to store park data, functions to display content, event listeners to respond to user actions, and conditional statements to show matching results.

Content

Home page

The home page will introduce the website and explain its purpose. It will include a hero image of a national park, a welcome message, and a short description of how the site helps users plan trips. It will also feature highlighted parks with brief summaries, such as Zion, Yellowstone, and Yosemite, along with buttons encouraging users to explore more. Images on this page will include a large banner image and smaller park photos. The page will also include a short section describing activities such as hiking, camping, wildlife viewing, and photography.

[Parks]

The Parks page will include a collection of park cards with information such as park name, location, best activities, and difficulty level. Each park will include an image and a short description. Example parks may include Zion National Park, Yellowstone National Park, Yosemite National Park, Grand Canyon National Park, and Rocky Mountain National Park.

This page will also use JavaScript to let users filter parks by activity or region. When a user clicks on a park card or button, more information will appear, such as travel tips, best season to visit, or popular attractions.
